How counselors can use MOS2Career

  1. Introduce the resource: explain that it is an optional, independent tool for civilian career exploration.
  2. Try it together: ask the participant to describe non-sensitive duties, equipment, leadership responsibilities and measurable accomplishments.
  3. Review possible jobs: compare suggested roles with current employer requirements and identify any missing credentials or experience.
  4. Turn exploration into action: use the résumé examples to build accurate achievement statements and create a follow-up job search plan.

Career suggestions are starting points for research, not guaranteed matches, job placements or certification determinations. Counselors should continue using official TAP, DOL and VA resources as applicable.

Safety, privacy and free access

Keep military information safe

Never enter classified, Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI), export-controlled information, sensitive operational information, Social Security numbers or other unnecessary personal details into public tools.

No payment required for these tools

The resources linked in this counselor guide are free to explore. MOS2Career may separately offer optional paid products; those are not required to access the tools listed here. See our privacy policy and terms.

Questions from transition offices

Is MOS2Career an approved TAP, DOL ENPP or SkillBridge provider?

No. This page is intended for resource evaluation and outreach. No official approval, affiliation or referral relationship is claimed.

Can our staff share the free tools with participants?

You may review and share publicly accessible links as an optional independent resource, subject to your office's own policies. This does not imply official endorsement.

Does a suggested civilian job mean the participant qualifies for it?

No. Participants must verify job postings, licenses, credentials and employer requirements themselves. The tools support planning, not placement.

How can our office request a walkthrough or share feedback?

Email support@mos2career.com with the installation or organization, your role and what you would like to review. Do not include sensitive participant information.
