Questions about the free Job Match Analyzer

What does the analyzer compare?

It compares the civilian posting with the military duties, skills, education, and credentials you choose to describe. Review the distinction between demonstrated experience, possible transferable skills, and qualifications that still need verification. Add specific, releasable examples rather than relying on your military job title alone.

Do I need an account or email address?

No account or email address is required for this free analysis. Enter your service branch, specialty, and a civilian job description; relevant duties and credentials help make the comparison more useful. Your inputs are sent to an AI service to produce the report, so leave out personal, classified, and sensitive information.

Does a strong match mean I qualify for the job?

No. The employer decides hiring qualifications, and the relevant regulator determines authorization for licensed work. Verify education, experience, current credentials, and the permitted scope of the role before applying or choosing training. Read the actual posting alongside the analysis.

How is this different from the Career Translator?

Use the free Career Translator to explore possible civilian career directions. Use this analyzer when you have a specific job posting to compare with your background. The military career guide library provides specialty examples and practical qualification checks.
