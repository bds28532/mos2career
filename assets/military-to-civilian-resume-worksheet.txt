Use this worksheet to draft an experience entry for a specific civilian job.
Replace every bracketed field. Include only work you did and facts you can
support. Keep all information unclassified and authorized for public release.

1. CHOOSE YOUR TARGET

2. GATHER YOUR EVIDENCE

Service / organization: [organization]
Dates: [Month Year to Month Year]
Tasks you personally performed: [tasks]
Tools or processes you used: [tools / processes]
Verified scope, workload, or result: [facts and where you can verify them]
Your responsibility: [performed / assisted / supervised / approved]
Relevant credentials, issuer, and current status: [details]

3. DRAFT YOUR BULLETS
Pattern: action + specific work + scope or verified result.

- [Action verb] [specific task or problem] using [tools or process you used].
- [Action verb] [work you owned] for [verified scope], resulting in
  [supported outcome].
- [Action verb] [team, customer, or training contribution] with
  [relevant context].

If you cannot support a number or outcome, describe the completed work
without adding one. Label course completions and active certifications
accurately. Keep your actual job title; do not claim a civilian position
you have never held.
