Army 25B Civilian Jobs and Career Paths

As an Army Information Technology Specialist, you may have helped users regain access, maintained workstations, supported networks, or kept unit systems operating in changing conditions. Those experiences can connect to civilian technical support and IT operations work.

The best Army 25B civilian jobs for you depend on the duties you performed. A support-heavy assignment can point toward service desk or desktop roles, while substantial server or network responsibilities may support a more specialized search.

Civilian career options for Army 25B veterans

The Army describes 25B work as supporting military computer systems, including hardware, software, networks, and customer services. Assignments and responsibility levels vary, so choose examples that reflect your own service. [1]

Service desk technician

This can be a practical match for experience helping users with account access, applications, peripherals, or routine connectivity problems. Show how you gathered information, documented troubleshooting, and followed through with the customer.

Qualification considerations: Education requirements vary across support jobs. Some accept a high school diploma with relevant IT certifications; others expect college study. Demonstrate customer communication and familiarity with the employer's support environment. [2]

Desktop support technician

Consider this direction if you installed, configured, maintained, or replaced user equipment. Examples might include preparing workstations, troubleshooting hardware, or verifying that devices and applications functioned before handover.

Qualification considerations: Match the posting's operating systems, endpoint tools, and support duties. Military device accountability is useful context, but it does not establish experience with a commercial endpoint management platform you have never used.

IT operations technician

This path may fit experience checking system status, responding to service interruptions, following recovery procedures, and handing unresolved issues to the next shift. Explain how you recognized a problem and what you did within your authority.

Qualification considerations: Compare the posting's monitoring tools, escalation procedures, and schedule with your experience. Network operations center positions may call for deeper network troubleshooting than a general unit support assignment provided.

Junior systems administrator

Consider this title when you can demonstrate actual server, directory, account, permissions, update, or backup responsibilities. Explain which actions you performed independently and which required approval or assistance.

Qualification considerations: Administration jobs typically require a bachelor's degree, although some accept a certificate or associate degree. Check platform requirements and the level of production responsibility. Supporting users on a system is different from administering that system. [3]

Network support specialist

This may fit practical work checking connectivity, cabling, network devices, or local network services. Explain the tests you used and how you separated an endpoint problem from a wider network issue.

Qualification considerations: Be ready for the routing, switching, addressing, or other knowledge specifically named in the posting. A 25B title alone does not establish experience designing networks or configuring every device type.

Cybersecurity support specialist or junior analyst

This can be a direction to investigate when your duties included relevant security checks, vulnerability remediation, access reviews, or incident support. Describe your actual work and the tools you can use, rather than relying on the word cyber in a unit name.

Qualification considerations: Security analyst roles typically require related experience and a degree, with alternative routes for some candidates. General IT experience can be a useful foundation, but you may need more practice with logs, alerts, investigations, and the employer's security tools. [4]

Army 25B resume examples

Keep Information Technology Specialist and Army service in your work history. If accurate, add a short descriptor such as end user support or systems support. Describe the civilian function in the bullets that follow.

These examples are illustrative. Replace bracketed fields with verified information and remove any duty you did not perform. Counts should describe your contribution rather than an entire organization's work.

  • Provided technical support to [number] users, troubleshooting account access, software, and peripheral issues and documenting actions in [actual tracking system].
  • Prepared [number] workstations for authorized users by installing approved software, checking connectivity, and confirming required functionality.
  • Maintained records for [number] IT assets and reconciled equipment handovers with the responsible personnel.
  • Assisted with [specific server or directory task] under [level of supervision], recording changes and verifying results with the administrator.

For example, replace Performed S6 duties with Supported unit users with computer, account, and connectivity issues, documenting work and coordinating escalation. Add tools and measurable outcomes when they are accurate and releasable.

Build the qualifications your target role needs

Prioritize demonstrable technical skills

For support roles, practice explaining a troubleshooting sequence from initial symptoms to resolution or escalation. For systems roles, document the account, server, or maintenance tasks you can perform. For network roles, demonstrate the fundamentals named in the posting.

A personal lab can show developing skills. Label it as a project, describe what you built or tested, and avoid presenting it as production experience.

Choose training after comparing job postings

A Cisco CCNA may be relevant to a networking target. CompTIA Security+ may be relevant to foundational security knowledge. Verify which credentials a specific employer requires, and check your existing certifications before paying for duplicate training. A certification does not establish all of the experience required for a role. [5] [6]

If a position has clearance or other eligibility requirements, read them separately from the technical qualifications and describe your status accurately.

Plan a focused 25B job search

  1. List the tasks you performed most often and the tools you can explain in an interview.

Frequently asked questions

What civilian jobs match Army MOS 25B?

Potential matches include service desk, desktop support, IT operations, network support, and selected systems roles. Security positions may also be relevant when you can demonstrate the required security work and technical skills.

Does every 25B qualify to be a systems administrator?

No. Your experience needs to match the role. Explain whether you administered accounts, servers, permissions, updates, or backups, and whether you performed those tasks independently or with supervision.

Do I need a degree or certification?

Requirements vary by position. Support roles can offer several entry routes, while more specialized roles may have stronger education or certification expectations. Compare required qualifications before choosing additional training. [2] [3]

Does 25B experience qualify me as a cybersecurity engineer?

The MOS alone does not establish that qualification. An engineering opening may require security design, implementation, automation, or other experience that your assignment did not provide. Compare the actual duties rather than matching on the word IT.

How should I explain S6 work on a civilian resume?

Describe the function, such as unit IT support, and specify your own duties. Explain who you supported, the issues you handled, the tools you used, and the results you can verify.

Can I get a remote IT job after serving as a 25B?

Some IT jobs offer remote work, while others require hands-on equipment support or work at a particular site. Search with your preferred work arrangement and check location, schedule, and equipment requirements for each opening.
