# MOS2Career | Military Transition Resource Evaluation Proposal

**Prepared for:** Military Transition Assistance Program coordinators, installation transition offices, employment navigators and veteran service organizations  
**Prepared by:** MOS2Career LLC  
**Website:** https://mos2career.com/  
**Contact:** support@mos2career.com  
**Status:** Request for resource review; not an official government partnership.

## Purpose
MOS2Career requests the opportunity to demonstrate its independently developed, free career exploration resources as an optional supplement for transitioning service members. Our goal is to help participants translate military duties into civilian-language skills, explore potential job directions, and prepare more accurate résumés. We are requesting a review or introductory discussion, not approval or endorsement in advance.

## Available free resources
- **Military Career Translator:** helps participants explore civilian career options based on military experience and stated skills.
- **Military Job Match Analyzer:** supports comparing a potential civilian job with an individual's background and identifying qualifications to verify.
- **Military-to-Civilian Résumé Examples:** provides practical examples of describing transferable experience.
- **Occupation-specific career guides:** cover selected Navy and Army ratings/MOS specialties, including information technology and logistics.

Free resource overview for counselors (draft, not published): https://mos2career.com/tap-counselor-resources/

## Proposed 20-minute walkthrough
1. Brief overview of the free tools, intended users and important limitations (5 min).
2. Demonstrate a fictional military background and potential civilian career exploration (10 min).
3. Discuss how staff could independently evaluate usefulness and provide feedback (5 min).

## Participant safeguards
The resources are optional, are not a substitute for required TAP curriculum or professional transition counseling, and do not guarantee employment, eligibility, credentials or pay. Users should not provide classified data, Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI), export-controlled information, Social Security numbers or sensitive operational details. We do not request participant information in this proposal.

## Evaluation and next steps
We welcome an initial review with no obligation for the office to use or recommend the service. With an office's agreement, MOS2Career can collect voluntary, privacy-conscious feedback on clarity, usefulness and areas needing improvement. Any more formal collaboration would require separate eligibility review and the appropriate government process.
